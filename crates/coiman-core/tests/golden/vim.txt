# Vim golden cases. Each case: "=== name", the text with | before the cursor
# char, "--- keys" (Vim notation), then the expected text and cursor.
# The test runs every case through Coiman IDE's engine and, when Vim is
# installed, through real Vim (vim -u NONE) to check the expectations.
# <sync> splits the keys into separate Vim commands (each its own undo step).

=== w moves to the next word
|foo.bar baz
--- w
foo|.bar baz

=== 3w counts words and punctuation
|foo.bar baz
--- 3w
foo.bar |baz

=== W skips punctuation
|foo.bar baz
--- W
foo.bar |baz

=== e goes to the end of the word
|foo bar
--- e
fo|o bar

=== b goes back a word
foo |bar
--- b
|foo bar

=== ^ goes to the first non-blank
  ab|c
--- ^
  |abc

=== j and j keep the column across a short line
abcdef
ab
ab|cdef
--- kkjj
abcdef
ab
ab|cdef

=== j keeps the wanted column past a short line
abc|def
ab
abcdef
--- jj
abcdef
ab
abc|def

=== G goes to the last line
one
|two
three
--- G
one
two
|three

=== 3G goes to line 3
|a
b
c
d
--- 3G
a
b
|c
d

=== } goes to the next blank line
|a
b

c
--- }
a
b
|
c

=== f then ; repeats
|a.b.c.d
--- f.;
a.b|.c.d

=== % jumps to the matching bracket
|if (x) { y }
--- %
if (x|) { y }

=== * searches the word under the cursor
|foo bar foo
--- *
foo bar |foo

=== / searches forward
|foo bar foo
--- /foo<CR>
foo bar |foo

=== dw deletes a word
hello |world foo
--- dw
hello |foo

=== dw on the last word keeps the line break
one.|two
three
--- dw
one|.
three

=== d2w deletes two words
|a b c d
--- d2w
|c d

=== de deletes to the end of the word
|foo bar
--- de
| bar

=== dt, deletes up to the comma
|abc,def
--- dt,
|,def

=== 2dd deletes two lines
a
|b

|d

a
b
|c
--- dd

|b

=== dj deletes two lines
|a
b
c
--- dj
|c

=== dk from the last line
a
b
|c
--- dk

=== D deletes to the end of the line
ab|cd
--- D
a|b

=== 3x deletes three chars
|abcdef
--- 3x
|def

=== x at the end of the line
ab|c
--- x
a|b

=== cw changes to the end of the word
foo |bar baz
--- cwxx<Esc>
foo x|x baz

=== ce changes to the end of the word
|foo bar
--- cexx<Esc>
x|x bar

=== C changes to the end of the line
ab|cd
--- Cx<Esc>
ab|x

=== s substitutes a char
|abc
--- sx<Esc>
|xbc

=== S changes the whole line
  |abc
--- Sx<Esc>
|x

=== ciw changes the inner word
say |hello there
--- ciwbye<Esc>
say by|e there

=== di( deletes inside parentheses
f(a, |b)
--- di(
f(|)

=== ct) changes up to the parenthesis
f(|abc)
--- ct)z<Esc>
f(|z)

=== ca" changes the quoted string and its trailing space
x = "he|llo" + y
--- ca"z<Esc>
x = |z+ y

=== yyp duplicates a line
|one
two
--- yyp
one
|one
two

=== xp swaps two chars
|abc
--- xp
b|ac

=== yiw then P pastes before the cursor
|foo bar
--- yiwwP
foo fo|obar

=== named registers
|one two
--- "ayiwwdiw"ap
one on|e

=== J joins lines
|foo
   bar
--- J
foo| bar

=== r replaces a char
|abc
--- rx
|xbc

=== 2~ toggles case
|abc
--- 2~
AB|c

=== >> indents with a tab
|foo
--- >>
	|foo

=== o opens a line below
|foo
bar
--- onew<Esc>
foo
ne|w
bar

=== O opens a line above
foo
|bar
--- Onew<Esc>
foo
ne|w
bar

=== A appends at the end of the line
|foo
--- A!<Esc>
foo|!

=== I inserts at the first non-blank
  |foo
--- I-<Esc>
  |-foo

=== 3i repeats the insert
|x
--- 3iab<Esc>
ababa|bx

=== . repeats dw
|a b c d
--- dw..
|d

=== . repeats an insert
|x
--- ihi<Esc>.
hh|iix

=== u undoes one change
|abc
--- x<sync>x<sync>u
|bc

=== u undoes a whole insert
|x
--- iab cd<Esc><sync>u
|x

=== Ctrl-R redoes
|abc
--- x<sync>u<sync><C-r>
|bc

=== v then d deletes the selection
|hello world
--- vwd
|orld

=== Vjd deletes the last two lines
a
|b
c
--- Vjd
|a

=== visual U uppercases
|abc
--- vlU
|ABc

=== v y then p
|ab
--- vly$p
aba|b

=== :s replaces on the line
|a a a
--- :s/a/b/g<CR>
|b b b
