# Helix-style (selection first) golden cases. Input uses | for the cursor;
# the expected text shows the selection in [brackets] (inclusive).

=== w selects the word and its trailing space
|foo bar
--- w
[foo ]bar

=== w then d deletes it
|foo bar
--- wd
[b]ar

=== e selects to the end of the word
|foo bar
--- e
[foo] bar

=== b selects back to the word start
foo ba|r
--- b
foo [bar]

=== x selects the line
|one
two
--- x
[one
]two

=== x then d deletes the line
|one
two
--- xd
[t]wo

=== c changes the selection
|foo bar
--- ecbaz<Esc>
ba[z] bar

=== y then p pastes after the selection
|ab cd
--- wyp
ab [ab ]cd

=== i inserts before the selection
|foo
--- ix<Esc>
[x]foo

=== a appends after the selection
|foo
--- eax<Esc>
foo[x]

=== r replaces every selected char
|foo bar
--- erx
[xxx] bar

=== ~ toggles case of the selection
|foo bar
--- e~
[FOO] bar

=== u undoes the change
|foo bar
--- edu
[f]oo bar

=== v extends with motions
|abcdef
--- vlll
[abcd]ef

=== gl goes to the line end
|abc def
--- gl
abc de[f]

=== f selects to the char
|abc,def
--- f,
[abc,]def

=== J joins lines
|foo
bar
--- J
foo[ ]bar

=== o opens a line below
|foo
--- obar<Esc>
foo
ba[r]
